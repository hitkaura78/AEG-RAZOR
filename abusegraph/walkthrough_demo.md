# AEG-RAZOR: 3-to-5 Minute Live Demonstration Runbook

This guide outlines the step-by-step presentation script and live actions for pitching **AEG-RAZOR (AbuseGraph)** to hackathon judges, merchants, and engineering leaders.

---

## 🛠️ Pre-flight Setup & Browser Layout

### 1. Launch Backend Server
In your PowerShell terminal, launch the server:
```powershell
$env:PYTHONPATH='.'
.\venv\Scripts\uvicorn backend.app.main:app --reload --port 8000
```

### 2. Arrange 3 Browser Windows (Side-by-Side or Tabs)
Open three browser windows:
- **Surface 1 (Customer UI)**: `http://localhost:8000/customer.html`
- **Surface 2 (Merchant UI)**: `http://localhost:8000/merchant.html` (Pre-filled: `merchant@demo.abusegraph` / `DemoMerchant123!`)
- **Surface 3 (Admin UI)**: `http://localhost:8000/admin.html` (Pre-filled: `admin@demo.abusegraph` / `DemoAdmin123!`)

---

## 🎬 Live Pitch Script (3-5 Minutes)

### Act I: Legitimate User Baseline — Zero-Friction Refund (1 Minute)

> **Speaker Pitch**: 
> *"First, let me show you what happens for a normal, honest shopper. Fraud prevention shouldn't punish good customers."*

1. **Actions**:
   - Go to **Surface 1 (Customer UI)**.
   - Click **Sign Up**, enter a new email (e.g. `alice@example.com`), and click **Register & Sign In**.
   - Select the **Everyday Backpack** ($49.99) and click **Order Now**.
   - Go to **My Order History**, find the order, click **Request Refund**, enter reason `"Item arrived damaged"`, and submit.
2. **Outcome to Highlight**:
   - The status immediately reads: **"Your refund is being processed" (`APPROVED`)**.
   - Point out that there is zero friction, zero risk terminology, and no delay for legitimate single orders.

---

### Act II: Coordinated Ring Detection & Real-Time Cross-Surface Sync (2 Minutes)

> **Speaker Pitch**: 
> *"Now, what happens when fraud syndicates operate in clusters sharing physical devices or network IPs? Watch how AEG-RAZOR flags abuse instantly across customer, merchant, and admin views without page refreshes."*

1. **Actions**:
   - Go to **Surface 3 (Admin UI)**, log in, and on the **Overview** tab click **Simulate Live Event**.
   - Switch to **Surface 1 (Customer UI)**:
     - The customer sees: **"Your refund is under review — our team will contact you" (`PENDING_REVIEW`)**.
     - Point out the strict customer privacy boundary: zero ML scores or internal risk jargon leaked.
   - Switch to **Surface 2 (Merchant UI)**:
     - Log in as the demo merchant. Within 3 seconds (live background polling), the **Pending Review (1)** badge illuminates.
     - Click **Inspect Evidence**: Show the judges the merchant evidence card:
       - **Risk Band**: Elevated Risk Band.
       - **Plain-language Reason Codes**: *"Multiple customer accounts share the same physical device fingerprint"*, *"Unusually high frequency of refund requests"*.
       - **Agent Narrative**: Written narrative generated by the AI investigation agent explaining the signals.
       - **Strict Field Omission**: Point out that raw floating-point ML vectors (`0.8523`) or graph nodes are strictly hidden from merchants.
     - Click **Accept Refund** and enter note `"Verified customer return"`.
   - Switch to **Surface 3 (Admin UI)**:
     - Open the **Risk Cases** tab:
       - Show the raw score breakdown (**ML Score: 0.8500**, **Graph Score: 0.75**, **Final Score: 0.8100**).
       - Show **Merchant Resolution**: `ACCEPT` • Note: *"Verified customer return"*.
       - Click **Inspect Detail**: Point to the **Full Audit Trail** showing the complete chronological sequence:
         $$\text{refund\_requested} \rightarrow \text{webhook\_received} \rightarrow \text{risk\_evaluated} \rightarrow \text{agent\_explanation\_generated} \rightarrow \text{policy\_evaluated} \rightarrow \text{merchant\_notified} \rightarrow \text{merchant\_decision}$$
     - Open the **Relationship Graph** tab:
       - Click **Reload Graph** to display the interactive `vis-network` topology.
       - Highlight the node color coding: **Indigo** (Normal Customers), **Rose** (Coordinated Ring Customers), **Amber** (Shared Devices), **Cyan** (Shared IPs).

---

### Act III: Model Evaluation, Policy Rules & Architecture Defense (1-2 Minutes)

> **Speaker Pitch**: 
> *"Finally, let's look under the hood at model performance and policy safety."*

1. **Actions**:
   - Go to **Surface 3 (Admin UI)** $\rightarrow$ **Model Evaluation** tab.
   - **Show Model Metrics**:
     - Point to **Precision (~0.90)**, **Recall (~0.90)**, **F1 Score**, and **PR-AUC**.
     - **Key Talking Point**: *"Notice that our precision is ~0.90, NOT a fake 1.00. Perfect 1.00 precision in synthetic demos indicates over-fitting or lack of noise. Our model is evaluated on noisy realistic features."*
   - **Show Charts**:
     - **Precision-Recall Curve**: Interactive Chart.js line chart showing trade-offs across thresholds.
     - **XGBoost Feature Importances**: Bar chart highlighting key signals (`order_velocity`, `graph_score`, `shared_device`, `avg_time_to_refund_hours`).
   - **Policy Isolation Rules Talking Point**:
     - *"Our policy engine enforces strict deterministic safety rules: A shared IP address alone (e.g. office building or campus Wi-Fi) or shared shipping address alone NEVER triggers auto-restriction."*
   - **Single-Service Architecture Defense Talking Point**:
     - *"Our backend uses an in-process NetworkX graph engine and FastAPI serving static files. We deliberately chose NOT to introduce Neo4j, Kafka, or microservices because our in-process engine evaluates multi-node graph clusters in under 10 milliseconds, completely eliminating network latency and operational complexity."*
